SDT zero is 5000000000-01-01T00:00:00.000Z on the proleptic Gregorian calendar, which is 157,784,697,832,780,800,000 ms after the Unix epoch. An SDT value is -x, where x is the milliseconds left until then.

JavaScript dates only reach the years −271,821 to 275,760, so SDT values outside that range convert to Unix time but have no calendar date here. Leap seconds are ignored, as in Date.
